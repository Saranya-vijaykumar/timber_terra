The Timber & Terra Story

From Single Timber Decks to Award-Winning Living Landscapes

Nearly two decades ago, Timber & Terra began with a single framing crew dedicated to honest woodworking in the rainy, demanding climates of coastal forests. We observed outdoor decks built as disposable afterthoughts—rotting joists, rusted fasteners, and warped planks within five years.

We redefined exterior engineering. By marrying traditional mortise-and-tenon timber joinery with modern laser-guided civil leveling, helical steel foundations, and marine-grade stainless hardware, we elevated outdoor decks into permanent architectural extensions of the residence.

Today, our interdisciplinary studio collaborates directly with luxury architects, landscape visionaries, and exacting homeowners to craft outdoor pavilions, cliffside cantilever decks, and sunken fire sanctuaries that endure generational weather cycles.

Every Timber & Terra exterior structure is fabricated in our temperature-regulated joinery facility before arriving on site. This controlled prefabrication ensures exact mortise tolerances, water shedding geometry, and flawless millwork finish.

Ecological Stewardship

Honoring the Forest with Every Board We Lay

We reject cheap composite plastics that leach micro-polymers into regional soil and prematurely crack under UV exposure. Our philosophy champions enduring natural hardwoods from certified sustainable forests, combined with non-toxic penetrating biological oils.

Carbon Negative Build Logic

Timber acts as a natural carbon sink. By building with structural timbers that last 50+ years, our installations maintain sequestered carbon far beyond average deck lifespans.
