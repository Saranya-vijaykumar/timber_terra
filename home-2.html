The 5 Pillars of Generational Outdoor Longevity

We engineer every joint, footing, and drainage line to endure high-salinity coastal squalls and sub-zero freeze-thaw shifts.

foundation

Torque Micro-Pile Bedrock Anchors

Unlike concrete footings that shift with soil erosion, our hydraulic torque-anchored steel piles seat directly into bedrock substrate with zero ground disruption.

handyman

Mortise & Tenon Timber Joinery

Master carpenters utilize Japanese interlocking joinery secured with structural oak dowels, allowing natural wood respiration without metal fatigue.

water_drop

Concealed Siphonic Drainage

Internal gutter channels integrated directly into perimeter beams harvest rain water silently and channel it away from structural load points.

shield

316-Grade Marine Stainless

Every screw, washer, tension rod, and balustrade bracket is machined from surgical-grade 316 marine stainless to prevent corrosion in coastal air.

air

PE-Stamped Seismic Connections

Our in-house structural engineers stamp every cantilever assembly to resist 120 mph wind uplift gusts and Zone 4 seismic lateral shear forces.
