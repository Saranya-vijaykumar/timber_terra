Architectural Philosophy

Where Organic Timber Meets Structural Precision.

Yes, completely turnkey. Our in-house drafting team prepares stamped engineering schematics, submits building permit applications to your municipal department, and compiles comprehensive architectural documentation packages for homeowner association (HOA) board reviews.
Permit review typically spans 2 to 4 weeks depending on jurisdiction. Once ground breaks, a standard 800–1,200 sq. ft. custom deck requires 3 to 5 weeks of on-site construction. Multi-level projects with built-in masonry kitchens and automated pergolas average 6 to 8 weeks.
Western Red Cedar offers unmatched organic aromatic beauty, tactile warmth underfoot, and natural resistance to decay, requiring a biennial oil application. Premium composites (such as TimberTech AZEK) provide zero annual sealing, lifetime stain warranties, and imperviousness to wet PNW moss conditions. We guide you through tactile samples in our initial consultation.
Our warranty covers all structural foundation piles, beam load integrity, ledger board anchors, stainless steel fastening hardware, and framing deflection exceeding 1/360th of the span. We also guarantee zero structural rot when maintained under our bi-annual maintenance protocol.
We operate on a transparent 4-tier milestone schedule: 15% upon design contract and permit filing, 35% on material delivery and framing mobilization, 35% on deck decking and pergola installation completion, and the final 15% only after the final client inspection and warranty deed handover.
