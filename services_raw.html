Frequently Answered Architectural Inquiries

Key considerations regarding Pacific Northwest climatic demands, engineering permits, and installation timelines.

The Pacific Northwest receives prolonged seasonal moisture and damp shade which accelerates fungal growth on standard pressure-treated woods. We combat this using dense exotic hardwoods (like Ipe and Cumaru) rich in natural anti-microbial oils, or ultra-resilient cellular PVC (such as TimberTech AZEK) which absorbs 0% water and is impervious to rot, mildew, and swelling.
Yes. Every project engineered by Timber & Terra includes complete end-to-end municipal permit acquisition. For complex hillside slopes, ravine setbacks, or multi-tiered decks exceeding 30 inches above grade, our in-house structural engineers stamp every blueprint and coordinate all geotechnical site reviews.
Yes, through rigorous structural sub-framing. We build dedicated reinforced joist spans and concrete cementitious backer-board heat deflectors under all barbecue grill suites, basalt stone islands, and fire tables to meet strict fire codes and prevent thermal deflection or frame sagging.
A standard custom architectural deck project requires approximately 2 to 3 weeks for architectural drafting and 3 to 5 weeks for municipal permitting. Once on-site construction begins, framing through hand-finishing spans 10 to 18 business days, depending on integrated electrical, gas lines, or pergola motorization packages.
